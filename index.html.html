FAQs

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

What is CWM® Certification? +
The Chartered Wealth Manager® is an American designation awarded by AAFM® USA, offered in India through AAFM India for wealth management and advisory careers.
Who is eligible? +
Anyone with a minimum 10+2 qualification can start the CWM® journey. A graduation degree is required to receive the final CWM® designation. Working professionals with 3+ years in finance/BFSI can appear directly for Level 2 via the Experience Pathway.
What is the exam format? +
Exams are MCQ-based, conducted monthly through Pearson VUE and NSE. Each level is 3 hours, 85 questions, with a 50% passing threshold and no negative marking.
Does CWM® guarantee a job? +
CWM® is a professional credential, not a job placement service. However, it significantly strengthens your profile across wealth management, advisory, and BFSI roles, and is recognized by employers seeking qualified wealth management professionals.
